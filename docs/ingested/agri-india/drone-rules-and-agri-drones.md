# Drones in agriculture (India): orientation note

Document type: Paraphrased orientation note prepared for this project. NOT the official rules.
Jurisdiction: India
Official source to verify: Civil Aviation Ministry, DGCA, and the Digital Sky platform (digitalsky.dgca.gov.in) for the Drone Rules, 2021 and later amendments or SOPs.
Status: NEEDS-VALIDATION. Drone rules, zone maps, and agricultural spraying SOPs are updated often.

## Main ideas
- Drone operation is regulated: the rules describe drone categories by weight, registration, remote pilot certification, airspace zones (such as green, yellow, and red zones), and operating conditions.
- The Digital Sky platform is the official channel for registering drones, managing permissions, and viewing the airspace map.
- Agricultural spraying has additional concerns: pesticide handling, drift near people, animals, water bodies and neighbouring crops, and operator training. Specific SOPs and approvals may apply. NEEDS-VALIDATION.
- Government programmes have promoted drone services through farmer groups and trained operators, so platforms may involve FPOs, self-help groups, drone operators, and service customers.

## Requirement prompts for a drone service platform
- How are operator certificates, drone registration, and permissions verified, and how are expiries handled?
- How are field boundaries geo-fenced, and how are no-fly or restricted zones checked before a mission?
- What flight, spray, and payload logs are recorded, and how long are they retained?
- How are chemical choice and dose approved, and by whom? The platform should not decide these without expert approval.
- What are the abort and emergency procedures, and how are incidents reported?
- What accuracy does GPS boundary data have, and what margin is used near field edges?
- How are customer consent, payment, and service reports handled?
- Safety-relevant control software calls for formal verification and testing evidence.

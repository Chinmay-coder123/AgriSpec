# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY): Official Operational Guidelines & Technology Mandates

**Document Title:** Revised Operational Guidelines for Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)  
**Authority:** Department of Agriculture, Cooperation & Farmers Welfare, Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India  
**Official Digital Channel:** National Crop Insurance Portal (NCIP) (pmfby.gov.in)  
**Jurisdiction:** Republic of India  
**Document Nature:** Official Operational Guidelines, Risk Coverage Norms, and Technological Assessment Standards  

---

## 1. Scheme Objectives and Scope

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) is India's national flagship crop insurance scheme aimed at providing financial support to farmers suffering crop loss/damage arising from non-preventable natural risks, stabilizing farmers' income, and encouraging the adoption of innovative agricultural practices.

---

## 2. Coverage of Risks and Insurance Stages

PMFBY provides comprehensive risk insurance coverage throughout the entire crop lifecycle:

### A. Prevented Sowing / Planting Risk
* **Coverage:** Insured area is prevented from sowing/planting/germination due to deficit rainfall or adverse seasonal/climatic conditions.
* **Claim Condition:** If more than 75% of the sown area in an insurance unit (IU) remains unsown or suffers germination failure, eligible insured farmers receive lump-sum financial assistance of **up to 25% of the sum insured**, and the insurance policy terminates.

### B. Standing Crop (Sowing to Harvesting)
* **Coverage:** Comprehensive yield loss coverage due to non-preventable natural risks:
  * Drought, dry spells, severe moisture stress;
  * Flood, widespread inundation, cloud burst;
  * Pests and plant diseases;
  * Landslides, earthquake, natural fire, and lightning;
  * Storm, cyclone, typhoon, tempest, and hurricane.
* **Basis of Assessment:** Area approach based on **Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs)** comparing the actual yield against the historical threshold yield (average yield of best 5 out of preceding 7 years multiplied by the indemnity level).

### C. Post-Harvest Losses
* **Coverage:** Assessment available for a maximum period of **up to 14 days from harvesting** for those specific crops which are required to be kept in **"cut & spread" / drying condition in the field** against unseasonal rains, cyclonic rains, or hailstorms.

### D. Localized Calamities
* **Coverage:** Individual farm-level assessment of loss resulting from the localized natural perils of:
  * Hailstorm;
  * Landslide;
  * Inundation (water stagnation causing root rot);
  * Cloud burst and natural fire.
* **Reporting Timeline:** Affected farmers or platforms must report the claim **within 72 hours of the occurrence** through the National Crop Insurance Portal, mobile app, or designated toll-free grievance number.

## 3. Technology Integration and Mandates

PMFBY mandates deep digital integration to minimize human subjectivity, prevent duplicate claims, and accelerate direct benefit settlement:

### A. National Crop Insurance Portal (NCIP - pmfby.gov.in)
* Acts as the single centralized database integrating:
  1. Financial institutions (banks, cooperative credit societies);
  2. Insurance companies;
  3. State Agriculture Departments;
  4. Farmers and Common Service Centers (CSCs).
* Requires mandatory de-duplication of farmer coverage using Aadhaar-seeded Farmer ID, Bank Account Number, and state Land Parcel Number (Khasra).

### B. Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs) via CCE-Agri Mobile App
* CCEs are mandatory field-level samplings conducted to calculate final average yield in each Insurance Unit (Gram Panchayat or Village).
* **CCE-Agri App Protocol:**
  * Mandatory live geo-tagging (latitude, longitude, altitude);
  * Strict date-and-time stamping;
  * Capturing live photographs of the marked 5m x 5m plot, harvested crop, and weight measurement;
  * Real-time automated synchronization to NCIP to eliminate post-facto data manipulation.

### C. YES-TECH (Yield Estimation System based on Technology)
* Implementation of advanced remote sensing and satellite analytics:
  * **Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI)** and **Normalized Difference Water Index (NDWI)** derived from Sentinel/ISRO satellite imagery;
  * Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) data for flood and inundation mapping during cloudy monsoon seasons;
  * Automated Weather Station (AWS) network feeds for rainfall, temperature, and humidity;
  * Machine learning models correlating satellite spectral signatures with historical CCE ground truth to resolve yield anomalies.

### D. WINDS (Weather Information Network Data Systems)
* Integration with hyper-local automatic weather stations installed at the block and Gram Panchayat levels to provide real-time parametric trigger data for weather-based crop insurance.

---

## 4. Claim Settlement and Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)

* **Mandatory Direct Transfer:** All insurance indemnity claim payouts must be disbursed directly to the farmer's Aadhaar-linked bank account via Public Financial Management System (PFMS) / National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) gateway.
* **No Middlemen or Cash Disbursements:** Claims can never be credited through agents, input dealers, or intermediaries.
* **Timeline:** Final indemnity claims must be processed and credited within **3 weeks** of receipt of certified yield data from the state government.

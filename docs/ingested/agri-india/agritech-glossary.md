# AgriTech and Indian agriculture glossary

Document type: Project-authored glossary for retrieval. Terms are described in general terms; verify specifics against official sources.

- Kharif: the monsoon-linked cropping season, with sowing around the start of the rains and harvest in autumn. Typical crops include rice, maize, cotton, and soybean.
- Rabi: the winter cropping season, sown after the monsoon and harvested in spring. Typical crops include wheat, mustard, and gram.
- Zaid: a short summer season between rabi and kharif.
- Mandi: a regulated or informal agricultural produce market where farmers sell to traders and buyers.
- APMC: Agricultural Produce Market Committee, the body that regulates mandis under state law.
- MSP: minimum support price, a government price announcement for selected crops.
- FPO: farmer producer organisation, a collective of farmers that aggregates inputs, produce, and services.
- Smallholder: a farmer with a small land area, often below two hectares.
- Tenant farmer or sharecropper: a farmer cultivating land they do not own, often missing from land records.
- Khasra, survey number, or plot number: identifiers for land parcels, which vary by state.
- Khata or record of rights: a land ownership or tenancy record, whose format varies by state.
- KCC: Kisan Credit Card, a credit product for farmers.
- eNWR: electronic negotiable warehouse receipt, used for pledging stored produce.
- Common service centre: a local kiosk that helps citizens use digital services.
- Extension officer: a government or institutional worker who advises farmers.
- KVK: Krishi Vigyan Kendra, a district-level agricultural science centre.
- Crop cutting experiment: a sample-based field measurement used to estimate yield.
- NDVI: a satellite or drone index that indicates plant greenness and vigour.
- Remote sensing: observation of fields from satellites or aircraft.
- Geo-tagging: attaching location coordinates to photos or records.
- Geo-fence: a virtual boundary used to limit where a drone or machine may operate.
- Precision agriculture: using sensors, data, and automation to apply inputs where and when needed.
- Fertigation: applying fertiliser through irrigation water.
- Drip irrigation: delivering water slowly to plant roots through tubes.
- LoRaWAN: a low-power, long-range wireless network commonly used for field sensors.
- Edge gateway: a small device that collects sensor data locally and forwards it when a connection exists.
- Cold chain: temperature-controlled storage and transport for perishable produce.
- Traceability: ability to follow produce from farm to buyer using batch and lot records.
- Offline-first: designing an app to work without a connection and sync later.
- USSD or IVR: phone-based channels that work on basic phones without internet.

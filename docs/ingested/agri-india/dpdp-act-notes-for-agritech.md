# Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: notes for AgriTech software

Document type: Paraphrased orientation note prepared for this project. NOT legal text and NOT legal advice.
Jurisdiction: India
Official source to verify: Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (meity.gov.in) and the Gazette text of the Act and the rules made under it.
Status: NEEDS-VALIDATION. Rules, timelines, and phase-in dates must be checked against current official publications.

## Key ideas in plain words
- Data principal: the individual the data is about, for example a farmer.
- Data fiduciary: the organisation that decides why and how personal data is processed, for example an AgriTech company, an FPO platform, or a government agency.
- Data processor: an organisation that processes data on behalf of a data fiduciary.
- Consent: generally needs to be free, specific, informed, and given by a clear affirmative action, with a notice that explains the purpose. Consent should be as easy to withdraw as to give.
- Purpose limitation and data minimisation: collect only what is needed for the stated purpose.
- Rights of the data principal: commonly described as access to information, correction and erasure, grievance redressal, and nominating another person to exercise rights.
- Security safeguards and breach intimation: the fiduciary is expected to keep reasonable security safeguards and to inform the regulator and affected people of a personal data breach as the rules specify.
- Children's data: special conditions apply, such as verifiable parental consent. A platform serving young farmers or family farms should check this.
- Significant data fiduciaries: some organisations may face extra duties because of the volume or sensitivity of data.
- Data Protection Board: the body described in the Act for handling complaints and enforcing the law.

## Why it matters for AgriTech
Farm products often collect names, phone numbers, bank details for payments, land parcel details, geo-tagged photos, device identifiers, and location traces. Combined, these can identify a person and reveal their livelihood.

## Requirement prompts
- Is there a plain-language consent notice in the farmer's language, with audio or icon support for low-literacy users?
- Is consent purpose-specific, logged with time and version, and withdrawable?
- Can the farmer see, correct, and delete their data? What is retained for legal or audit reasons?
- Are shared devices and assisted use (a family member, an FPO agent, or a kiosk operator helping a farmer) handled with clear identity and consent rules?
- Is location data collected only when needed, and is its precision appropriate?
- What is the breach response process and who approves notifications? NEEDS-VALIDATION with legal counsel.
